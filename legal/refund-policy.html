Last Updated: 08 September 2026

At Healthy Bharat Mission, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. This Refund Policy explains the conditions under which refunds are provided for products purchased from our store.

1. Introduction

This Refund Policy applies to all physical products, digital products, and services purchased through the Healthy Bharat Mission website.

2. Eligibility for Refund

You may be eligible for a refund if:

  • You receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect product.
  • The product is unused, unopened, and in its original packaging.
  • You raise a refund request within the specified time period (see below).

3. Non-Refundable Items

The following items are not eligible for a refund:

  • Products that have been opened or used (for hygiene and safety reasons).
  • Digital products, consultations, or programs that have already been accessed.
  • Items purchased during special sales or promotional offers (unless defective).

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact our support team with the following details:

  • Order number
  • Reason for the refund
  • Photos of the product (if damaged or incorrect)

You can reach us at support@healthybharatmission.org or through our Contact Us page.

5. Refund Process

Once we receive your request, our team will review it and get back to you within 2-3 business days. If approved, the refund will be processed to your original payment method.

6. Refund Timeline

Refunds are usually processed within 5-7 business days after approval. The time taken for the amount to reflect in your account may vary based on your bank or payment provider.

7. Return Shipping

In case of a defective or incorrect product, we will provide a prepaid return label. For other cases, return shipping costs may be borne by the customer.

8. Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, please inform us within 7 days of delivery. We will arrange a replacement or refund at no extra cost to you.

9. Subscription & Program Fees

Fees paid for consultations, programs, or subscriptions are non-refundable, unless stated otherwise at the time of purchase.
