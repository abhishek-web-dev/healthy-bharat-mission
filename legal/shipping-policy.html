At Healthy Bharat Mission, we strive to deliver your orders in a timely, safe, and reliable manner. This Shipping Policy provides details about our shipping process, delivery timelines, charges, and other important information related to the delivery of our products.

1. Introduction

This Shipping Policy applies to all physical products purchased through the Healthy Bharat Mission website. By placing an order, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

2. Shipping Coverage

We currently ship across India. We do not offer international shipping at this time.

3. Order Processing Time

Orders are typically processed within 1-2 business days after payment confirmation. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays will be processed on the next business day.

4. Delivery Time

Once dispatched, orders are usually delivered within 3-7 business days, depending on your location. Delivery times may vary due to logistical constraints, weather conditions, or unforeseen circumstances.

5. Shipping Charges

  • Free shipping may be available on selected orders or during promotional periods.
  • A standard shipping fee, if applicable, will be shown at checkout before you place your order.
  • Shipping charges are non-refundable.

6. Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking link via email or SMS to monitor your shipment status.

7. Delays

While we make every effort to ensure timely delivery, delays may occur due to factors beyond our control such as weather, regional restrictions, or courier service issues. We appreciate your understanding in such cases.

8. Damaged or Lost Packages

If your order arrives damaged or is lost in transit, please contact us within 7 days of the expected delivery date. We will investigate the issue and arrange a replacement or refund as applicable.

9. Incorrect Address

Please ensure that your shipping address is accurate and complete. We are not responsible for delays or failed deliveries due to incorrect or incomplete address details provided by the customer.
